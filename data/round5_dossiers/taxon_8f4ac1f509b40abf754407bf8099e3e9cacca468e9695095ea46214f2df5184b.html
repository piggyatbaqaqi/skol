Notes – Dictyosporium was established by Corda (1836), with D. elegans as the type species. 
Members of the Dictyosporium are mostly saprobes found on dead wood and plant litter in 
terrestrial and aquatic habitats (Ho et al. 2002, Hyde & Goh 1998, Pinnoi et al. 2006, Pinruan et al. 
2007, Shu et al. 2024, Yang et al. 2018). There are 88 epithets listed in Index Fungorum (2024), 
some of which have been transferred to similar genus Dictyocheirospora based on phylogeny or 
morphology (Boonmee et al. 2016, Yang et al. 2018).
