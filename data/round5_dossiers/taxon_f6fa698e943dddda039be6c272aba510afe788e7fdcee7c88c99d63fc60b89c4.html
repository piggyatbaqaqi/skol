Notes – Diaporthe sojae has a wide host range and geographic distribution (Udayanga et al. 
2015, Dissanayake et al. 2015, Norphanphoun et al. 2022). The sexual and asexual morphs of this 
species are described by Lehman (1923) and Udayanga et al. (2015). In the phylogenetic analysis of 
the concatenated alignment, our isolate (SICAUCC 23-0107) formed a cohesive cluster with D. 
sojae, supported by a robust 100% MLBS / 1.00 BYPP within the section Sojae (Fig. 50) 
(Dissanayake et al. 2024). Morphologically, the new collection exhibits characteristics closely 
resembling with D. sojae. A comparison of the ITS, tef1-α, tub2, cal, and his3 gene regions 
between SICAUCC 23-0107 and FAU635 revealed high sequence similarity, with similarity values 
of 99.64% (548/550 bp, 1 gap), 98.22% (332/338 bp, 2 gaps), 100% (440/440 bp, 0 gap), 100% 
(508/508 bp, 0 gap), and 99.79% (472/473 bp, 1 gap), respectively. Therefore, we identified our 
new collection as D. sojae, which is the first report on Phalaenopsis aphrodite.
