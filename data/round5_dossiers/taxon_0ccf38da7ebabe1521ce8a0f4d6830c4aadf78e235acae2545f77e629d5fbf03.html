description paragraph 1117   196912:198527  Description
Saprobic on Nayariophyton zizyphifolium woody litter. Sexual morph: Stromata solitary, scattered or gregarious, well-developed, consisting of black, elliptical or circular spots, moderately raised through epidermis with slit-like openings on the host surface. Ascomata 311–524 μm high, 430–607 μm diam. (x̅ = 455 × 527 μm, n = 10), perithecial, immersed, erumpent, globose to mostly subglobose, ostiolate. Peridium 23–45 μm wide (x̅ = 36 μm, n = 20), outer layer composed of dark brown textura angularis cells merging with the host tissues, inner layer composed of hyaline to subhyaline cells of textura angularis. Hamathecium comprises 1.8–3.6 μm diam. (x̅ = 2.6 μm, n = 30), with abundant, compact, hyaline paraphyses, occasionally branched, septate, tapering towards the apex. Asci 109–192 × 14–23 μm (x̅ = 155 × 17.7 μm, n = 20), unitunicate, 8-spored, cylindrical to cylindric-clavate, with short pedicel, rounded at the apex, with a doughnut-shaped, refractive, J-, apical ring. Ascospores 42–55 × 6.3–9.6 μm (x̅ = 49.2 × 8.2 μm, n = 30), overlapping, uniseriate or mostly biseriate, aseptate, claviform with a narrow pedicel, hyaline, guttulate, straight or slightly curved. Asexual morph: Undetermined. Culture characteristics – Ascospores germinated on PDA within 24 h and germ tubes emerged from one side. Colonies on PDA reaching 1–2 cm diam. after 14 days at room temperature (25–28 °C), circular, with entire margin, dense, pigment diffusing in the agar with age, upper surface orange in center and yellowish-white at outer zone, from reverse dark brown and black.

notes paragraph 1123   198962:200039  Notes
Known distribution – China (Xu et al. 2020), Thailand (this study). Notes – In the phylogenetic analyses, our strain (MFLUCC 24-0536) grouped with the holotype of Claviformispora phyllostachydis (SICAU 16-0007) with 100% ML and 1.00 PP statistical support (Fig. 43). Xu et al. (2020) introduced Claviformispora as a new genus to accommodate C. phyllostachydis, which was reported from dead branches of Phyllostachys heteroclada in China. Our new isolate is similar to C. phyllostachydis (SICAU 16-0007), except for the larger ascomata (x̅ = 455 × 527 μm vs. x̅ = 189 × 345 μm), asci (x̅ = 155 × 17.7 μm vs. x̅ = 118 × 13 μm), and ascospores (x̅ = 49.2 × 8.2 μm vs. x̅ = 45.7 × 7.0 μm) (Xu et al. 2020). Nevertheless, based on nucleotide comparisons, C. phyllostachydis (MFLUCC 24-0536) is identical to C. phyllostachydis (SICAU 16-0007) in SSU (0%), and no significantly difference in tef1-α (0.8%). Thus, the current study presents a new host record of C. phyllostachydis on Nayariophyton zizyphifolium, and a new geographical record from Thailand.
