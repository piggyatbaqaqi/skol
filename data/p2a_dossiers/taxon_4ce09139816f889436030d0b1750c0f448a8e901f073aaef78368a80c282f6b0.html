diagnosis paragraph 53   9770:11275  Diagnosis
In the protologue of Strobilomyces annamiticus, Patouillard (1909) cited two localities that correspond exactly with the localities of the collections Eberhardt LBA 161 and Eberhardt LBA 209. Eberhardt LBA 161 is designated here as lectotype. Singer (1945) regarded the two Vietnamese collections as immature basidiomata of B. emodensis, and S. annamiticus was synonymized with B. emodensis. With our re-examination of the syntypes and field observation on other collections, the present authors are convinced that the basidiomata of the two Vietnamese collections are the same as immature basidiomata of B. emodensis, because the size of basidiospores of B. emodensis may be variable at different stages of basidioma development. For example, the basidiospores of one immature basidioma (HKAS 59215) are [20/1/1] 15–18(–19) × 5–7 μm, with Q = (2.14–)2.29–3.17(–3.20) and Qm = 2.73 ± 0.28. They are clearly much shorter and narrower when compared to mature collections.
