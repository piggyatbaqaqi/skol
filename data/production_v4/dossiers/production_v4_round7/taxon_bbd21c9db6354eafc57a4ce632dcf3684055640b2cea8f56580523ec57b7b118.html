notes paragraph 2623   459577:460066  Notes
J. Lange (1935-40) treated this species as a Camarophyllus, despite his statement that the trama of the gills is almost parallel, but somewhat interwoven. He regards it as a transition to Hygrocybe. This species appears to be very rare in North America. It is close to H. acutus, but readily distinct as is pointed out in our discussion of that species. Its relationships appear to be with H. ovinus, but is not likely to be mistaken for either that species or H. nitratus.
