  • description
    Description.Prothallus not seen. Thallus grey with brown punctae, endolithic, contiguous conspecific thalli separated by dark lines. Perithecia 0.15–0.2 mm, 3/4(–1)-immersed, leaving deep pits in the rock; ca. 80–240 perithecia/cm2. Involucrellum absent. Exciple ca. 0.21–0.24 mm in diam., wall pale brown to medium brown, apex thickened to ca. 40–50 mm thick. Ascospores 0-septate 16–24 × 10–13(–14) mm.

    Notes.The species is rather similar to V. transfugiens, but has a paler exciple wall and slightly larger spores.
