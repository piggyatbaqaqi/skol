# `corpus_vocabulary.txt` — provenance and threshold

4 270 forms, one per line.  Generated by
[`bin/corpus_vocabulary.py`](../bin/corpus_vocabulary.py) from the
SKOL corpus itself.

**Half of the production D8 vocabulary**; the other half is
[`botanical_latin_wordlist.txt`](botanical_latin_wordlist.txt).

## Provenance and licence

Derived from `skol_exp_production_v4_02_00_treatments_prose` — our own
extracted corpus.  No third-party licence applies to the word list
itself.  The underlying treatments are extracted from published
literature under their respective terms; a list of bare word forms
carries none of that content.

## Method

Out-of-vocabulary alphabetic forms of 4+ characters from
`description` and `diagnosis`, counted by **distinct document**, kept
at `df >= 50`.  The 2026-08-21 run: **45 599 treatments scanned,
308 111 distinct forms, 4 270 above threshold**.

Published dictionaries do not carry the descriptive neo-Latin this
corpus lives on — Whitaker's *WORDS* has neither `basidiomata` nor
`campanulatus` at any age — but the corpus does, by construction.

## The threshold is the safety argument

The corpus contains OCR-corrupted treatments, so a naive vocabulary
would absorb their corruption: the `vere`-for-`were` hazard, where a
junk token in the word list masks the damage being hunted.  Two
defences:

* **Counting documents, not occurrences.**  A form repeated forty
  times inside one mangled treatment still counts once.
* **`df >= 50`.**  A form must recur across fifty independent
  documents before it is believed.

**Lower is not safer.**  Measured across the threshold:

| df ≥ | forms | Latin cov. | corrupt-Latin | taxon_43a7b19e | worst poster child |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 99 806 | 97.6 % | 16.00 % | 13.62 % | **6.59 %** |
| 5 | 37 332 | 96.3 % | 21.33 % | 16.45 % | 1.23 % |
| 25 | 8 280 | 76.8 % | 26.67 % | 20.43 % | 1.23 % |
| **50** | **4 270** | 63.4 % | 22.67 % | **22.19 %** | **0.00 %** |

At `df >= 2` the vocabulary is large enough to manufacture spurious
rejoins and the worst poster child reaches 6.59 %.

## Known limitation

Being corpus-derived, this list is **not pure Latin**.  Its
unique-to-corpus forms include English technical terms (`abaxial`,
`abhymenial`, `abscission`), French (`abondantes`, `abord`) and
truncation debris (`abun`, `acad`).  That is tolerable for a rejoin
metric — those are real strings that appear in the corpus — but it is
why the botanical Latin list, verified against a printed source, is
paired with it rather than replaced by it.

Regenerate from a §9-mode-B-filtered corpus once D8 itself exists, so
that corrupted treatments stop contributing at all.

## Reproducing

```sh
bin/corpus_vocabulary --experiment production_v4 \
    --output data/corpus_vocabulary.txt
```

Requires the `wamerican` package for `/usr/share/dict/american-english`
(override with `--english-wordlist`).  `--threshold` defaults to 50.
